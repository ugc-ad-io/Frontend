Delete your account

UGCad · Android app com.ugcapp · Last updated 2 September 2026

You can ask us to delete your UGCad account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains how to request it, what gets removed, and what we are legally required to keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@ugcad.io from the address registered on your account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. Include the email address or username of the account to delete.

Email us to delete your account

We send a confirmation to the email on the account before anything is deleted, so that nobody else can close your account. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

Before you request deletion

Settle anything open first. If you have funds in your wallet, money held in escrow, an active deal, or an unresolved dispute, we will contact you to close these out before the account is deleted. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile: name, contact details, bio, category, rates and profile photo
  • Portfolio photos and videos you uploaded
  • Your messages and in-app chat history
  • Notification preferences and app settings
  • Saved briefs, bids and draft campaigns

What we must keep, and for how long

Some records cannot be deleted on request because Indian law requires us to retain them. These are locked to compliance use only and are not used to identify or contact you.

DataWhy we keep itHow long
Financial records Transactions, invoices and payouts must be retained under Indian tax and accounting law As required by law
KYC records PAN and Aadhaar verification records must be retained under anti-money-laundering rules As required by law
Dispute records Evidence needed to resolve a dispute and any appeal Until resolved and the appeal window closes

Content you delivered to a brand under a completed deal stays with that brand under the usage rights agreed in the brief. That is part of the contract between you and them, and is not affected by deleting your account.

Other options

If you do not want to lose your account, you can instead edit or remove individual details from your profile in the app, or turn off notifications in the app's settings. Email us if you would like a copy of your data rather than deletion.

Questions

Contact support@ugcad.io. For how we handle your information generally, see our Privacy Policy.
