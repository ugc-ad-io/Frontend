Support

UGCad · Help with your account, payments and deals

Something not working, or a question about a deal? Email us and a person will read it.

Email support support@ugcad.io We reply within 1–2 business days, Monday to Friday.

To help us answer faster, include the email address on your account, and the campaign or deal ID if your question is about one. Screenshots help for anything visual.

Common topics

Signing in

Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password" on the login screen to get a reset code. If you signed up with Google, use the Google button rather than a password.

Verification (KYC)

Creators verify identity with PAN and Aadhaar before withdrawing earnings. You can browse, bid and message without it. If a submission was rejected, the reason appears on the KYC screen.

Wallet, escrow and payouts

Brands add funds to a wallet, and the agreed amount is held in escrow when a creator is selected. It is released to the creator once the work is approved. Creators withdraw to a bank account or UPI. Email us if a transaction looks wrong or a payout has not arrived.

Campaigns, bids and deliverables

Brands post briefs and creators bid. Deliverables, timelines, revision limits and usage rights are set in the brief and binding once the deal starts. Revisions are requested from the deal screen while work is under review.

Disputes

Either side can raise a dispute on an active deal. Work pauses while our team reviews and may ask both parties for evidence. Creators should try to resolve with the brand first, then escalate to an admin from the chat if that does not work.

Messaging

Messages stay on the platform so escrow, disputes and evidence all work. Sharing phone numbers or email addresses in chat is blocked for that reason, and repeated attempts can restrict an account.

Reporting someone

If a brand or creator breaches our guidelines, report them from the chat or email us with the details. We review every report.

Account and data

  • Edit your profile any time from Account Settings in the app.
  • Change what you receive under Notification Settings.
  • To close your account, see Delete your account.
  • To request a copy of your data, email support@ugcad.io.

Reporting a bug

Send us these and we can usually reproduce it straight away:

  • What you tapped, and what happened instead of what you expected
  • Your phone model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 14, iOS 18)
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of Settings
  • A screenshot or screen recording
